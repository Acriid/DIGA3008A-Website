The fourth prototype was about macro design which lead to the following hypothesis: "Will the introduction of a 
rogue-like level generation complement the horror themes of the game or will it remove the player from the immersion 
of the game." The main focus of this prototype was the procedural generation algorithm which would create the level
for the player to play in. It was a basic Binary Space Partitioning algorithm with a key and door placed randomly
on the map. The enemy AI chased the player on this map.
