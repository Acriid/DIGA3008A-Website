The third prototype was about micro design of the moment to moment gameplay which lead to the following hypothesis:
"How will the change of a horror aesthetic to a greybox aesthetic affect how the player interacts with the game 
when the game has a horror game enemy AI and other horror elements?" This lead to the main focus of this prototype
which is the enemy AI. The enemy AI was inspired by the Alien from Alien:Isolation. The hypothesis was better because
I could prove the hypothesis through how the mechanics and different graphics of the game interact with the player.
