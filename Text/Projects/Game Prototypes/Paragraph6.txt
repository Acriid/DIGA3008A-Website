The exam prototype was to choose any of the other prototypes and improve them. I decided to improve each prototype
while mainly focusing on the procedural generation of the fourth prototype. The hypothesis of this prototype was:
"Will the addition of mechanics that allow the player to traverse the map in different ways
allow the use of bigger maps or will it cause the player to be frustrated with the map despite
the extra mechanics to traverse the map." The focus was on improving the procedural generation by adding hidden paths
between rooms along with teleporters that teleport the player from one room to another. This allowed for the map
to be bigger while also allowing more ways for the player to run from the enemy AI.
