Cult game is a 3D game made in unity. The game was originally made for an assignment where a group of 3 people needed
to make the game over the course of a semester where parts of the game are submitted during the course of development,
while the entire game was submitted as an exam. Cult game is about a detective investigating the cult in Cult City.
The detective gets kidnapped by the cult in order for them to summon their god. The player takes control of the detective
and must try to stop the ritual while trying to escape the cult.
