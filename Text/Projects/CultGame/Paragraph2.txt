When the player loads into the game they will see a cutscene explaining the story to them so far. The player will then
wake up in an abandoned warehouse where they will need to pick up a ritual book. The ritual book will allow the player
to see messages hidden to the naked eye while also allowing the player to go where they are not supposed to. During the
game the player will actually be completing the ritual instead of stopping it due to the influence of the book that
the player picked up. The player will also find different items that allow them to see that which is not usually seen.
